Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do I own? ▼

A fractional share of a managed layer unit. You don’t own a specific bird — you own the right to the production from that unit for the year.

How are eggs delivered? ▼

Weekly collection points in major towns or home delivery in Nairobi. You can also convert to supermarket vouchers.

Is this regulated? ▼

Kukusave operates as an agribusiness savings product. We partner only with licensed farms and follow Kenyan consumer protection and data laws.
